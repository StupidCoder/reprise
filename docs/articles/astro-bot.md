## Local research and limits

Reviewed the local Beacon README (Decomp/Beacon/README.md): it identifies the
PS4 game, CUSA12392 v1.04, and describes an incomplete flat-screen reconstruction.
No separate game.md or plan.md was found there. These implementation reports
are not used as evidence of original development decisions. No code, asset
map, external reverse engineering or disassembly was consulted for this article.
The article concerns the original PS VR game; it promises no Reprise VR support
or integration coverage. It does not conflate Rescue Mission with Astro's
Playroom or the later Astro Bot game.
