# Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker — Small worlds, big discoveries

Body: `resources/articles/captain_toad_3ds.txt`. Seven paragraphs. Research checked
1 November 2026. Original prose, no direct quotations or borrowed illustrations.

## Paragraph-to-source map

- P1 and P3: [Alexa Ray Corriea, GameSpot, Nintendo Changed Captain Toad to Avoid Zelda Similarities (2014)](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/nintendo-changed-captain-toad-to-avoid-zelda-simil/1100-6423612/).
  Full relevant interview passages read. Hayashida explains compact stages,
  jumping restrictions, Hiratake's clay models and choosing the Galaxy character;
  Hiratake explains forward-facing Shy Guy awareness. This is creator testimony
  about design, not new reverse-engineering evidence. Under 200 derived words.
- P2 and P4: [Thomas Whitehead, Nintendo Life, interview with Shinya Hiratake and Koichi Hayashida, 9 December 2014](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2014/12/interview_captain_toad_treasure_trackers_developers_talk_over_origins_and_the_contents_of_toads_backpack).
  Full relevant Q&A read. Hayashida explicitly corrects the Zelda-origin story:
  Mario diorama tests, Link proposal stopped at planning, Galaxy character,
  3D World stages, Miyamoto's standalone request. Hiratake supplies the
  more-than-100-stage trial, plucking action and three-episode progression.
  Under 200 derived words; stage count describes experiments, not shipped levels.
- P5: [Nintendo Miiverse interview with Shinya Hiratake, reproduced by NintendObserver (February 2015)](https://nintendobserver.com/2015/02/marichans-captain-toad-miiting-with-shinya-hiratake-on-miiverse/).
  Republished interview text read, especially the question about ideas that did
  not make the game. It explicitly describes first-person experiments for every
  stage and their reuse for minecarts and turnip cannons. Original Miiverse is
  retired; this is a mirror of primary testimony, not a live original page.
- P6: [Nintendo Everything, 7 August 2018, quotations from Polygon's interview](https://nintendoeverything.com/captain-toad-devs-on-decision-to-bring-the-game-to-switch-and-3ds-future-more/).
  Full quotation blocks read. [Original Polygon interview URL](https://www.polygon.com/2018/8/7/17658584/captain-toad-treasure-tracker-nintendo-legend-of-zelda)
  returned 502 during this research; the article explicitly attributes the
  reproduced account. Claims concern motivations and sequence, not production
  dates, port staffing or an inference that the 3DS edition has co-op.
- P7: [Nintendo UK, 3DS product page](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-3DS-games/Captain-Toad-Treasure-Tracker-1348072.html).
  Product description, 13 July 2018 release date and 2DS/3DS stereoscopic caveat
  read. Final sentences are editorial interpretation, not developer quotation.
